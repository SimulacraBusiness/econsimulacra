**EconSimulacra** is a simulation platform for studying complex socio-economic
systems with large language model (LLM) agents.  The framework enables
researchers and practitioners to simulate household consumption, firm pricing
strategies, narrative diffusion through social networks, and spatial mobility
of agents.
